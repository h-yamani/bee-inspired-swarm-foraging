# Research Background

## Persian research title

شبیه‌سازی جستجوی غذا در رباتیک ازدحامی بر اساس الگوریتم کلونی زنبورهای مصنوعی

## Research question

How can the division of labour, recruitment, and distributed search behaviour of honey-bee colonies be adapted to a swarm of mobile robots so that food is collected efficiently while unnecessary robot motion—and therefore energy expenditure—is reduced?

## Core design ideas

- decentralised local control rather than a shared global planner;
- scout robots for discovering food sources;
- localised communication of source position and value;
- recruitment of a limited number of foragers;
- resting behaviour for robots not currently needed;
- return-to-nest navigation and food deposition;
- obstacle avoidance and unsuccessful-search recovery; and
- experimental comparison across controller and swarm configurations.

## Indexed record

The University of Kashan/Elmnet record identifies the work as a 1391 master’s thesis by Hoda Yamani in the Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering, supervised by Hossein Ebrahimpour-Komleh and indexed with advisors Ahmad Yoosofan and Morteza Babamir.

The associated conference paper was published in the 11th National Conference on Intelligent Systems under Civilica document `ICS11_122`. Its abstract reports reduced energy waste, improved efficiency relative to an adaptive foraging method, and scalability with increased arena and robot numbers.
